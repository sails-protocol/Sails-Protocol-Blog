# Editorial policy

This blog is not normative protocol documentation.

IDEA → DRAFT → TECH REVIEW → CLAIM REVIEW → EDITORIAL REVIEW → BUILD/LINK CHECK → PREVIEW → PUBLICATION APPROVAL → PUBLISH.

Claims concerning custody, escrow, settlement, security, economic authority, exactly-once semantics, production readiness and external integrations require direct evidence and CTO approval. Planned features must not be presented as deployed. Every technical diagram requires adjacent explanatory text and technical verification.

NO MATERIAL FINDING UNDER THE RUG.

WebMCP read-only article discovery is a Day-0 release blocker. Agents must not receive write, publish or delete capabilities. Browser-level registration and execution evidence are required.
